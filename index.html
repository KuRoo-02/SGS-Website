The ground station

Purpose-built for mission-critical satellite operations.

Our Kuala Lumpur gateway (KL2) sits 68 km from KLIA and 80 km from the metropolitan core — far enough for a clean RF environment, close enough for engineering access. It is linked to the TSGI ground station (KL1) by 65 km of dark fibre operating in full redundancy.

  • Antenna farm supporting C-band, Ku-band and Ka-band operations
  • Dual telecommunications providers — Telekom Malaysia and Fiberail
  • Redundant power: utility feed, generator and battery plant
  • Dedicated telco room, server rooms and main switchboard rooms
  • On-site weather station and 24/7 monitored operations

Why SGS

Satellite networks need more than bandwidth.

They need dependable ground infrastructure, operational expertise and a partner able to respond to the technical demands of mission-critical communications.

Carrier-neutral by design

No preferred upstream. Operators, carriers and enterprises interconnect on equal terms with multiple fibre providers on site.

GEO and LEO under one roof

Hybrid architectures combining geostationary reach with LEO latency — including hybrid router solutions for resilient links.

Backed by APStar

A strategic alliance partner under APT Satellite Holdings, giving direct access to established regional fleet capacity.

Licensed and accountable

MCMC individual licences as NFP and NSP, plus ASP(C) registration — a regulated counterparty for long-term hosting.

Engineering depth on site

Installation, integration, commissioning, maintenance and remote support delivered by the team that runs the station.

Positioned for growth

Capacity and floor space to scale with cloud, OTT and IoT traffic, plus lower-CAPEX options using refurbished VSAT hardware.
